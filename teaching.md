<!--Teaching is my first love, and I came to linguistics through the English as a Second Language (ESL) Teaching program at the University of Utah.I find real passion in teaching, and work to improve my teaching and mentoring abilities as junior faculty at Western Washington University.-->I am grateful for the students who I have been able to learn alongside, and find passion in improving my pedagogical practice. During my graduate studies I was fortunate to learn the skills of teaching through mentorship from senior instructors and practice as both a teaching assistant and instructor of record. I actually discovered the field of linguistics while completing the English as a Second Language Teaching certificate program at the University of Utah! As an undergraduate, I was a student teacher of Intermediate ESL and Content ESL Math and Science at Cottonwood High School in Salt Lake City, Utah.  I also was a teaching instructor for language and assimilation tutors at Catholic Community Services of Utah.

I am currently an Assistant Professor at Simon Fraser University, and teach courses in the Linguistics department and Social Data Analytics program, teaching all tenures of students. I was a Visiting Assistant Professor of linguistics at Western Washington University for the 2024-2025 academic year, teaching various linguistics courses at the undergraduate level. As a graduate student at UW, I was a TA for various Linguistics and Computer Science courses.
